1. Summary

  • This policy covers only the stage-A test: the test build distributed through Google Play internal testing in Korea, and tester recruitment and observation sessions. For the public release and later stages, see section 6.
  • No accounts or login. The app does not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Game progress, settings and the date of birth you enter at first launch are stored on this device (Android backup and device transfer: section 2), and this build is set up not to send them to us or to any other company. Details are in section 3.
  • The library files for usage analytics, automatic crash reporting, ads and paid purchases are included in the app, but this build is set up not to turn these features on (not to start or initialise them) (section 3).
  • Google Play and Android communicate independently of the app (installation, updates, security checks) and we do not control that (section 3), so we do not claim that no information is processed at all.
  • Stage-A observation sessions are held in person, on company-owned test phones only. We do not collect testers' personal Google account emails and do not add them to the Play Console tester list. If you join a test, we process your name (or the name you want to be called) and contact details, observation notes, questionnaire answers and, if you agree, a screen recording (section 4). We explain this and ask for consent before you take part.
  • We do not sell personal information.

3. Kinds of communication: the app's own, Google Play / Android, and external pages you open

A. The app's own communication

  • This build (stage A) is set up so that the app itself does not send data (usage records, crash reports, identifiers, the advertising ID, location, contacts, etc.) to our servers or those of any other company.
  • What we checked in the app code and build settings: the game code has no function that sends requests to a server. The library files for remote analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase), automatic crash reporting (Crashlytics), remote configuration (Remote Config), ads (AdMob) and in-app billing are included in the app, but this build is set up not to start them, and the components that would start them automatically, the advertising-ID permission and the billing permission are removed in the build settings. Notifications are scheduled on the device; no server push is used (section 2).
  • This is based on a review of the app code and build settings and on emulator tests. Such checks are observations on specific devices at a specific time, not a guarantee for every device or situation.

B. Google Play / Android communication (separate from the app)

  • Joining the test, downloading and updating the app through Google Play, Google Play services and security checks such as Google Play Protect, and Android operating-system functions (backup and device transfer, showing notifications, etc.) are carried out by Google and the device maker independently of the app. We do not control this processing; it follows the Google Privacy Policy and the device maker's policy.
  • In Play Console we can see statistics that Google aggregates and provides to developers, such as install counts, device types and crashes. We do not use them to identify individual testers.

C. External web pages and email you open yourself

  • When you tap "Privacy policy" on the app's Settings > Privacy screen, your device's web browser opens this policy page (https://onetry-co.github.io/privacy/). The page is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA); the access information GitHub processes when the page opens is described in section 5-C. We have not put cookies, analytics tools or external scripts on this page. Other links in this policy (such as the Google Privacy Policy) also open only when you tap them.
  • When you tap "Write a deletion request" on the same screen, your device's mail app opens with the recipient (privacy@onetry.co.kr), subject and body filled in (the app version and, if statistics were ever turned on in an earlier test version and the IDs from then are still on the device, those analytics IDs (app-instance ID and Firebase installation ID)). The app does not send the email; it is sent through your mail service only if you send it from your mail app. Emails we receive are kept as in section 7.

The app uses no web cookies, no Android advertising ID and no precise location.

Analytics IDs, two separate points: (1) The stage-A build does not create new analytics identifiers and does not use any identifier to track users. (2) However, if an analytics ID created by an earlier test version (one in which statistics were turned on) is still on the same device, that ID may be included in the deletion-request email. We use it only to find and delete the data collected during that earlier test.

5. Processors and transfer abroad

A. The game app's own transmission: none

The stage-A build is set up so that the app itself does not send data to our servers or those of any other company (section 3-A). So there is no entrustment of personal information to processors and no transfer abroad by us in connection with use of the app. In stage A no tester data is entered in Play Console.

B. External services the company uses

Separately from the app, we use a company email service to answer privacy inquiries and to contact testers, and we entrust the storage, sending and receiving of that email to its provider (entrustment of processing). In short, there is one entrustment: the company email service; we do not entrust personal information to anyone else or transfer it abroad (for the hosting of this policy page, see section 5-C).

6. Not covered by this policy (later stages)

Usage statistics (opt-in), crash reporting, ads, paid purchases and distribution to the general public are not part of stage A and are outside the scope of this policy. Before distributing a build that turns any of them on, we will publish a new policy and ask in the app for any required consent before the feature first starts. If a device with the stage-A build is updated to such a version, the updated app will tell you what changed on its first launch.

8. Your rights and how to exercise them

You may ask at any time to access, correct or delete your personal information, to stop processing, or to withdraw consent. You may also stop taking part in a test at any time without giving a reason and without any disadvantage.

  1. Tester data: contact us (section 12) and we will find and delete, or let you see, your recruitment data, notes, recording and answers. Data already aggregated so that no one can be identified cannot be singled out.
  2. On-device data: we do not hold it; delete it yourself by uninstalling the app or via Android Settings > Apps > Storage > Clear data.
  3. Data processed by Google (your own Google account, Play install history, device backup): we do not hold it; requests must go to Google.
  4. We handle requests within the deadlines and by the procedures set by law, and in principle we tell you the result or the progress within 10 days of receiving your request. For anything we cannot do, we give the reason and how to object.
  5. A legal guardian or authorised representative may make requests; we may check the authority as needed.

9. Children and teens

The game is rated for all ages. Observation testers are recruited only among people aged 19 or older. The age check (date of birth) is used only on the device, and the stage-A build is set up not to send usage information regardless of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information of children under 14, and we do not carry out any processing that requires a legal guardian's consent without that lawful consent. If we receive personal information of a child under 14 (e.g., by email) we delete it promptly after replying.

For kids (in simple words)
· Your birthday and game progress are saved on this phone. We made the game so it does not send them to us.
· Deleting the game deletes them too. The game is also set to be left out of phone backups and moving to a new phone, but on some phones it may still be moved. If you have questions, ask a grown-up to contact us with you.

10. Security

  • Minimal collection: the app is set up not to send usage information, and testers give only the items in section 4.
  • The linking table is kept separately (CEO only), and notes use participant numbers only.
  • We apply access controls to the devices and accounts that handle tester data (including company email, Play Console and company test phones).
  • Deletion as in section 7 when the retention period ends.

11. Automatic collection

The stage-A app uses no web cookies or similar tracking (advertising ID, analytics identifiers). The Google Play / Android communication in section 3-B and the external pages in 3-C are not something we install or operate in the app.
